Emirates Rubyx Credit Card FAQs
What are the ways to get an Emirates Rubyx Credit Card?
You can apply online through the ICICI Bank website or by visiting an ICICI Bank branch.
Does Emirates Rubyx Credit Card offer international airport lounge access?
No, the Emirates Rubyx Credit Card offers 1 complimentary domestic lounge visit per quarter. and is available only on a spend of ₹75,000 in the preceding quarter.
How can I avail discount on Emirates flight bookings using my ICICI Bank Emirates Rubyx Credit Card?
To avail upto 10% discount on Emirates flight bookings, please follow the below steps
Visit www.emirates.com or open the Emirates App.
Click on “Book Now”
Enter promo code “INICI26” before searching for flights.
Complete your booking using your Emirates Skywards ICICI Bank Rubyx Credit Card at the time of payment.
How can I redeem the Skywards Miles earned?
You can redeem the miles for flight bookings, upgrades, hotel stays, and more through Emirates and its partners.
Does Emirates Rubyx Credit Card offer an annual fee waiver?
No, the annual card charges cannot be waived. However, the complimentary Emirates Skywards Blue Tier Membership gets automatically renewed after the annual fee payment.
What is the criteria for being eligible for the movie benefits offer with BookMyShow?
Starting Apr 1, 2026, to be eligible for availing complimentary tickets offer on movie/ events tickets with BookMyShow, you must spend ₹25,000 or more on your Emirates Rubyx Credit Card within the preceding spends quarter. This entitles you to avail the offer in the following calendar quarter. Please go through the illustration below to understand spends and offer eligibility period.
Please go through the below illustration to understand the spends period along with the lounge eligibility period:
Spends period offer -  eligibility period
Spend ₹25,000 between Dec 26, 2025 and Mar 25, 2026 - Get movie offer benefit between Apr 01, 2026 and June 30, 2026.
Spend ₹25,000 between Mar 26, 2026 and June 25, 2026 - Get movie offer benefit between July 01, 2026 and Sep 30, 2026.
Any additional bookings beyond the complimentary benefit will be charged as per the rates decided by the service provider. To book your movie ticket and for detailed terms & conditions, please click here .
Add-on/Plus card members need to individually spend ₹25,000 in the preceding spends quarter, to be eligible for the offer in the subsequent calendar quarter
Is there any cashback offer on American Express Credit Card variant while booking movie tickets on BookMyShow?
Cashback offers on the Amex variant have been revoked effective from 01 Jan 2025.
Customers can avail upfront offers while booking tickets on BookMyShow by following the steps below-
Navigate to ‘Apply Offers’ section
Click on the tab - Credit/ Debit/ Net Banking
Choose ICICI Bank Credit Card 25% Discount Offer on selected ICICI Bank Credit Cards
Enter the Card Number and click ‘Apply Offer’
Once the success message shows up, the offer will be applied on the billing page.
What are the entertainment and lifestyle benefits of this card?
You can avail of discounts while dining out in select restaurants and movie ticket bookings.
How is the maximum credit limit for the Emirates Rubyx credit card determined?
The credit limit is determined by the bank and depends on several factors like your credit score, income, and repayment history.
Who is this card best suited for?
You are a frequent Emirates flyer and want to earn Skywards Miles on your spending, but are looking for an affordable credit card with valuable benefits.
