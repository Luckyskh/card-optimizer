Frequently Asked Questions
What is the renewal charge of the HDFC Bank INFINIA Metal Edition Credit Card?
The renewal fee for the HDFC Bank INFINIA Metal Edition Credit Card is ₹12,500 plus applicable taxes, charged annually. However, if you spend ₹10 lakh or more in the preceding 12 months, the fee is waived. Click here for more details.
What is the joining fee for the HDFC Bank INFINIA Metal Edition Credit Card?
A ₹12,500 joining fee, plus applicable taxes, is charged for the HDFC Bank INFINIA Metal Edition Credit Card at the time of application.
What is the over-limit fee for HDFC Bank INFINIA Metal Edition Credit Card?
The HDFC Bank INFINIA Metal Edition Credit Card charges an over-limit fee of 2.5% of the over-limit amount, subject to a minimum of ₹550. This fee is applicable when your card usage exceeds the assigned credit limit.
What is the minimum amount payable on HDFC Bank INFINIA Metal Edition Credit Card?
The HDFC Bank INFINIA Metal Edition Credit Card requires a minimum payment of 5% of the total outstanding balance or ₹200, whichever is greater.
What is the dynamic currency conversion markup fee for HDFC Bank INFINIA Metal Edition Credit Card?
The HDFC Bank INFINIA Metal Edition Credit Card applies a 2% markup fee on all foreign currency transactions, covering conversion rates and international processing costs.
What is the fuel surcharge waiver for HDFC Bank INFINIA Metal Edition Credit Card?
The HDFC Bank INFINIA Metal Edition Credit Card offers a 1% fuel surcharge waiver at all fuel stations across India. This waiver amount is capped at ₹1,000 per statement cycle.
What is the railway ticket purchase fee for HDFC Bank INFINIA Metal Edition Credit Card?
The HDFC Bank INFINIA Metal Edition Credit Card imposes a 1% fee on railway ticket purchases, plus GST.
What are the finance charges for both revolving credit and cash advances for HDFC Bank INFINIA Metal Edition Credit Card?
The HDFC Bank INFINIA Metal Edition Credit Card levies a finance charge of 1.99% per month (23.88% annually) on both revolving credit and cash advances, applicable to the outstanding balance until fully paid.
What are the rent payment charges for HDFC Bank INFINIA Metal Edition Credit Card?
The HDFC Bank INFINIA Metal Edition Credit Card charges a rent payment fee of 1% of the transaction amount plus GST. However, the maximum fee is restricted to ₹3,000.
What is the reissue of charges for lost or damaged cards for HDFC Bank INFINIA Metal Edition Credit Card?
The HDFC Bank INFINIA Metal Edition Credit Card imposes a reissue fee of ₹100 in case of a lost or damaged card.
