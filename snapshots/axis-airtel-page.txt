Documents for Airtel Axis Bank Credit Card:
Unless there are case variations, the required documents for Airtel Axis Bank Credit Card application include a copy of your PAN card or Form 97, residence proof, identity proof, a colour photograph and proof of income in the form of latest payslip / Form 16 / IT return copy.
Keep your documents for Airtel Axis Credit Card ready
