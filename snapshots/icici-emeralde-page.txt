ICICI Bank Emeralde Credit Card FAQs
What are the benefits offered with the Emeralde Credit Card?
The Emeralde Credit Card gives you benefits such as the following, and many more.
Buy one ticket and get up to ₹750 off on the second ticket, up to 4 times in a month through BookMyShow
Unlimited international and domestic airport lounge access
Up to 4 golf rounds every month, basis spends on the Card
What birthday benefits are offered with the Emeralde Credit Card?
Eligible Emeralde Credit Card customers can receive a ₹5000 birthday benefits voucher on eligible American Express variant cards, subject to the applicable terms and conditions.
To know more about redemption of vouchers, click here
For other terms and conditions,  click here .
How to avail Complimentary International Airport lounge access?
Enjoy unlimited complimentary access to international airport lounges with ICICI Bank Emeralde Credit Card. Complimentary benefits are applicable only for the primary Cardholder.
For an ICICI Bank Emeralde Credit Card customer who has applied for the card on or after 15 November 2024.
Priority Pass Membership
Complimentary Priority Pass Membership for the primary Cardholder with ICICI Bank Emeralde Credit Card
Access 1,400+ lounges in 145+ countries worldwide
Present your Priority Pass Membership Card at the participating international lounges.
For complete list of international airport lounges visit https://www.prioritypass.com/airport-lounges
What are the criteria for availing domestic lounge access?
For lounge visits within India, the primary Cardholder can avail unlimited complimentary lounge access using their ICICI Bank Emeralde Credit Card. Please swipe the ICICI Bank Emeralde Credit Card to avail the complimentary lounge access. To view the complete list of lounges, click here .
What is the criteria for being eligible for the movie benefits offer with BookMyShow?
Starting Apr 1, 2026, to be eligible for availing complimentary tickets offer on movie/ events tickets with BookMyShow, you must spend ₹25,000 or more on your Emeralde Credit Card within the preceding spends quarter. This entitles you to avail the offer in the following calendar quarter. Please go through the illustration below to understand spends and offer eligibility period.
Please go through the below illustration to understand the spends period along with the lounge eligibility period:
Spends period offer - eligibility period
Spend ₹25,000 between Dec 26, 2025 and Mar 25, 2026 - Get movie offer benefit between Apr 01, 2026 and June 30, 2026.
Spend ₹25,000 between Mar 26, 2026 and June 25, 2026 - Get movie offer benefit between July 01, 2026 and Sep 30, 2026.
Customers with Credit Card on dual networks (Mastercard & Amex | Visa & RuPay) can avail one movie offer per month, per variant. Any additional bookings beyond the complimentary benefit will be charged as per the rates decided by the service provider. To book your movie ticket and for detailed terms & conditions, please  click here .
Add-on/Plus card members need to individually spend ₹25,000 in the preceding spends quarter, to be eligible for the offer in the subsequent calendar quarter
Is there any cashback offer on American Express Credit Card variant while booking movie tickets on BookMyShow?
Cashback offers on the Amex variant have been revoked effective from 01 Jan 2025.
Customers can avail upfront offers while booking tickets on BookMyShow by following the steps below-
Navigate to ‘Apply Offers’ section
Click on the tab - Credit/ Debit/ Net Banking
Choose ICICI Bank Complimentary tickets offer on selected ICICI Bank Credit Cards
Enter the Card Number and click ‘Apply Offer’
Once the success message shows up, the offer will be applied on the billing page.
What are the golf privileges and the eligibility criteria to avail them?
As an Emeralde Credit Cardholder, you are eligible for a round or lesson of golf for every ₹ 50,000 spent on retail purchases in the previous calendar month. You can avail a maximum of 4 rounds per month. The benefit can be booked through the Golftripz website. Simply visit  https://teetimes.truztee.com/  and create a Golftripz account using the same e-mail ID as registered for your ICICI Bank Credit Card. Benefits will be credited to your account by the 10th day of the following month, available for use in the next two months.
To view the updated list of golf courses, please  click here . For complete details of the golf programme and the associated terms and conditions, please   click here . You may also call iAssist, the 24/7 concierge service on 1800 26 70731 (toll free for MTNL or BSNL lines) or 022 67872016 (standard call charges applicable) for assistance with online booking.
How do I make the booking for Golf Rounds?
Eligible ICICI Bank Credit Card members can book their Golf Rounds by following the below steps:
Log in to the ICICI Bank Golf platform https://teetimes.truztee.com/ with your registered mobile number.
Choose your preferred golf course and select your preferred date and tee time.
Submit your booking request and await confirmation.
Check your Booking History to view all bookings and details.
You may also call i-Assist, our 24X7 concierge service on 1800 26 70731 (toll free for MTNL/BSNL lines) or 022 6787 2016 (standard call charges applicable), for assistance with online booking. An authorisation amount of ₹2 shall be taken for each eligible Card.
How to redeem Credit Card points for rewards?
You can experience seamless redemption of reward points with ICICI Bank. First, log into ICICI Bank Net Banking > Cards > Credit Cards > Redeem or the ICICI Bank's iMobile app iMobile > cards & forex > Select Credit Cards > Redeem, you can redeem your ICICI Bank reward points across multiple categories such as electronics, lifestyle, luxury, shopping and travel.
Who should use this Card?
The Emeralde Credit Card is an ideal Card for people who enjoy a luxurious lifestyle. You get unlimited international and domestic lounge access for the primary Cardholder, a shopping voucher from TATA CLiQ worth ₹5,000 (only American Express Cardholders) and ICICI Bank reward points.
Can I make an online purchase with the Emeralde Credit Card?
Yes, you can easily make online purchases across India and in select overseas online stores using the Emeralde Credit Card.
What is the age limit to apply for the Emeralde Credit Card?
You must be between 21–65 years of age to be eligible to apply for the ICICI Bank Emeralde Credit Card.
What Air Accident insurance coverage is provided to Cardholders?
The primary Cardholder is entitled to Air Accident insurance coverage of ₹ 3 crore. This benefit applies only when the Card has been used at least once in the 60 days prior to the incident. Note that the insurance covers international travel only, provided that the tickets were purchased using the Credit Card. Supplemental Cardholders are not eligible for this insurance coverage. For more details on the terms and conditions of the insurance cover on ICICI Bank Credit Cards, please  click here .
What are the key features of the concierge services offered?
The concierge service provides 24/7 support for varied needs such as restaurant reservations, flower and gift deliveries, hotel and flight bookings, car rentals and even movie ticket delivery. Additionally, the service offers emergency auto assistance and medical concierge support, ensuring comprehensive assistance during emergencies. Contact i-Assist for access to these services. To contact the i-Assist team, please call 1800 26 70731 (toll free for MTNL/BSNL) or 022 67872016 (standard call charges applicable).
What fraud protection benefits are available to Cardholders?
Cardholders are protected against fraudulent transactions with Counterfeit Card Liability, ensuring coverage against unauthorised usage. Additionally, the Card provides an
Account Takeover Cover, which safeguards you in case your personal information is accessed or misused by a third party without your consent.
What is the fuel surcharge waiver offered to Cardholders?
Cardholders can avail of a 1% fuel surcharge waiver on all fuel transactions of up to ₹ 4,000 at any fuel outlet in India. This benefit applies to both the primary Cardholder and the Plus (supplemental) Cardholder, helping them to save on regular fuel expenses. The waiver is automatically applied when the eligible transaction is completed using the Credit Card.
