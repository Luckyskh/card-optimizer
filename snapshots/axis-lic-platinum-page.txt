Documents for LIC Axis Bank Platinum Credit Card application:
Unless there are case variations, the required documents for LIC Axis Bank Platinum Credit Card application include a copy of your PAN card or Form 97, residence proof, identity proof, a colour photograph and proof of income in the form of latest payslip / Form 16 / IT return copy.
