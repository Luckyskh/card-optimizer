Frequently asked questions
What is an HSBC RuPay credit card?
RuPay was developed to offer an affordable, secure, and local alternative to international card networks like Visa and Mastercard. It combines the words 'Rupee' and 'Payment' to show it comes from India.
HSBC RuPay credit cards can be used online, in store, and at ATMs all over India. They can also be linked to a UPI ID, to enable safe and secure instant payments.
How do I use my RuPay credit card for UPI?
To get started, add your HSBC RuPay credit card to the HSBC SimplyPay app, or any third-party UPI app.
How to add your RuPay card in the HSBC SimplyPay app:
Open your SimplyPay app
Open ‘My account’ in the menu
Select ‘Add account’
How to add your RuPay card in a third-party app:
Open your UPI app
Search for HSBC Bank in the Bank list
Select your HSBC RuPay credit card, then ‘Confirm’
Set your 6-digit UPI PIN
Start using your credit card on UPI apps
For more information about HSBC SimplyPay, visit HSBC SimplyPay .
Can I use my HSBC RuPay credit card internationally?
Yes, you can make transactions in around 160 countries and regions worldwide through the JCB network.
