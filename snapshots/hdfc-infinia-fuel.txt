Fuel Transaction
Convenience fee waiver of 1% on all fuel transactions. The waiver on convenience fee is
applicable only for fuel transactions between Rs. 400 to Rs. 1,00,000 (Maximum waiver of INR
1000 per statement cycle)
Fuel surcharge varies from 2.5% to 1% of fuel transaction amount. The rate of surcharge may
vary depending on the fuel station and their acquiring bank. GST is as applicable.
