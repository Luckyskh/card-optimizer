Dear Customer,
Greetings from IDFC FIRST Bank.
We are reaching out to inform you of upcoming changes to the charges associated with non-maintenance of the required Average Monthly Balance (AMB) in your Corporate Salary Account.
IDFC FIRST Bank Salary Account will remain a Zero Balance Account as long as the salary is getting credited regularly. If the salary is not getting credited into the account for 3 consecutive months, then you are required to maintain the Average Monthly Balance (AMB).
Details of upcoming changes
Non-Maintenance of AMB : Effective 1 st January 2025, a fixed percentage charge will apply for failing to maintain the required AMB in your Corporate Salary Account. This charge will be based on the shortfall between the actual balance maintained and the minimum balance required, as agreed at the time of account opening.
