Lounge Access
A. Domestic Lounge Access – VISA / RuPay Card
W.e.f 10-Jun-2025, Domestic Lounge Access benefit will be Milestone based and can be availed as a Lounge Voucher on spends of Rs 50,000 or more in a calendar quarter. Please note, you can access 2 Complimentary Domestic Lounge Vouchers per calendar quarter (up to 8 per calendar year). Click Here for the list of eligible domestic lounge access.
Upon qualifying for the benefit, you will receive SMS and Email, intimating you about the qualification. Please visit Claim Portal and login using your Bank registered mobile number and claim your lounge voucher within 120 days of qualification. Once you have claimed the voucher, you can use the voucher in the eligible lounge list within 180 days of issuance of the voucher, for more details Click here . You can also resend/view your claimed vouchers on Claim Portal.
To check the spends and qualification status in the current calendar quarter, from 10-Jun-2025 you can login into HDFC Bank Netbanking/Mobilebanking app > Go to Credit Card Section > Select Tata Neu HDFC Bank Credit Card > Click on Redeem Reward Points (you will be re-directed to Rewards Portal) > In Rewards Portal Go to MyReward > Spend Promo Details.
Note:- If your card is upgraded, lost, or reissued spends done on only new card will be considered. Spends done on previous card will not be considered for qualification.
B. International Lounge Access – VISA Card
W.e.f 01-Jun-2025, Priority Pass on Tata Neu Infinity HDFC Bank Credit Card- VISA will be issued only basis request. In case you have been issued a new/ renewed/ replacement card, then you can apply for Priority Pass for self and add-on members by calling HDFC Bank phonebanking toll-free numbers 1800 1600 or 1800 2600 to enjoy the international lounge access as per the product feature benefit.
Using Priority Pass, you and your add on member can together avail up to 4 Complimentary Lounge Access per calendar year (1 per Quarter), outside India.
If you exceed the 4 Complimentary (1 per Quarter) visits, you will be charged at US $27 + GST per visit
All guests visits using Priority pass would be charged at US $27 + GST per visit per guest
Please note: Priority Pass in India will attract charges on your Credit Card. Click here to know more. You can visit www.prioritypass.com for list of lounge details.
To know about your lounge access history
Please write a mail to pp@prioritypass.com.hk. Please provide name, complete 18 digit Priority Pass number and state that PP Membership is via HDFC Bank program. If there is more than 1 Priority Pass number (belonging to add-on PP holders), then please provide all the PP Numbers.
C. International Lounge Access - RuPay
4 Complimentary Lounge Access per calendar year (1 per Quarter), outside India. Customers will be charged a transaction fee of $3.25 on their Credit card towards complimentary lounge access, same shall be reversed. Visit exceeding the complimentary quota will be allowed at the discretion of lounge and will also be chargeable by the lounge.

NeuCoins Validity and Redemption
Tata NeuCoins Claim and Redemption Process on Tata Neu HDFC Bank Credit Card
Effective 25-Nov-2026 , to use your Tata NeuCoins earned on Tata Neu HDFC Bank Credit Card spends, primary card holder has to claim them through the Tata Neu App.
How to claim your Tata NeuCoins from your Tata Neu HDFC Bank Credit Card
1. Log in to the Tata Neu App using primary card holder’s Bank-registered mobile number
2. Go to NeuCard section
3. Select ‘ Claim NeuCoins’ in NeuCard section . Complete the steps as prompted.
Note: - The Closing Tata NeuCoins Balance as per your latest statement will be available for claiming within 7 working days of the statement date, please refer to the example for better understanding.
4. Tata NeuCoins earned on your Tata Neu HDFC Bank Credit Card can be claimed within 12 months from the statement date in which it was earned . Unclaimed Tata NeuCoins will expire after this period.
Example
If your statement is generated on 15-Dec-26 , any additional Tata NeuCoins earned in the latest statement will be updated in the claimable Tata NeuCoins Balance latest by 22-Dec-26 .
Tata NeuCoins earned in the statement generated on 15-Dec-26 can be claimed until 14-Dec-27 from your credit card account. If the Tata NeuCoins are not claimed by 14-Dec-27 , then the Tata NeuCoins will expire.
How to use your Tata NeuCoins post credit to your Tata Neu Membership Account
5. Once your claim is successful, the Tata NeuCoins will be credited to your Tata Neu Membership account . The claimed Tata NeuCoins will be valid for another 12 months from the date of credit of Tata NeuCoins in your Tata Neu Membership Account , after which they will expire as per the Tata Neu Rewards Program Terms & Conditions.
Note: - It may take up to 7 working days to credit the Tata NeuCoins in your Tata Neu Membership Account.
6. Tata NeuCoins available in your Tata Neu Membership Account can be redeemed across eligible Tata brands within the Tata NeuCoins validity period.
7. For details on the redemption and validity of Tata NeuCoins after they are credited to your Tata Neu Membership Account, please click here .
Example
Tata NeuCoins claimed from your credit card account and credited to your Tata Neu Membership Account on 15-Dec-26 , will be valid until 31-Dec-27 in your Tata Neu Membership Account.
For another example, if Tata NeuCoins are credited to Tata Neu Membership Account on 27-Dec-26 , they will be valid until  31-Dec-27.
For more details kindly click here .
Notes:
1-Effective 25-Nov-2026 , Tata NeuCoins earned on your Tata Neu HDFC Bank Credit Card will no longer be automatically credited to your Tata Neu Membership Account.
2- The primary Cardholder can make up to 3 successful requests per day to claim Tata NeuCoins. Each claim must have a minimum value of 100 Tata NeuCoins . The minimum claimable value per claim may be revised downward from time to time. Any revised minimum claimable value will be displayed to the customer at the time of making the claim.
3- Tata NeuCoins earned during special promotions or as Welcome Benefit will continue to follow the terms, and the expiry communicated at the time of issuance.
How the Tata NeuCoins earned on Tata Neu HDFC Bank Credit Card statement prior to 25-Nov-2026 will be credited to the Tata Neu Membership Account?
As per the existing process the Tata NeuCoins earned on Tata Neu HDFC Bank Credit Card statement prior to/ before 25-Nov-2026 will be credited to the Tata Neu Membership Account within 7 working days of the statement generation and will have a validity of 12 months (including any lock period) from the end of the month in which they're credited to your Tata Neu Membership Account. Example:
If Tata NeuCoins are credited on 05-Aug-26, they will be valid until 31-Aug-27.
If Tata NeuCoins are credited on 31-Aug-26, they will be valid until 31-Aug-27.
How to redeem Tata NeuCoins from Tata Neu?
Once your Tata NeuCoins are successfully claimed and credited to your Tata Neu Membership Account, you can continue to use them for purchases on the Tata Neu App/Website at 1 Tata NeuCoin = ₹1 savings , just as you do today, across eligible Tata brands, including:
Air India Express
Bigbasket
Croma, Westside.
Tata CLiQ, Tata CLiQ Luxury
Hotel Bookings/Purchases on IHCL
TATA 1MG
Qmin
Titan and Tanishq (only via Tata Neu)
You can use your Tata NeuCoins by selecting Tata NeuCoins /Use my Tata NeuCoins/ Loyalty Redemption on order page/payments page. Tata NeuCoins can only be used on eligible transactions as defined by the individual brands.
For offline redemption of Tata NeuCoins on selected stores kindly click here .
